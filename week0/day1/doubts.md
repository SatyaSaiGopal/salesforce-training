# Salesforce Day 1 Notes

## What is Salesforce?
Salesforce is a cloud-based CRM platform used to manage customer relationships.

## What is CRM?
CRM stands for Customer Relationship Management.

## Objects
Objects are database tables used to store information.

Example:
- Lead
- Contact
- Account

## Record
A record is one row inside an object.

## differnce between salesforce admin and developer?
salesforce admin: manages and configures salesforce and no coding.
salesforce developers: helps in building the functionality and applications.
