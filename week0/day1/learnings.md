# 🔹 What is Salesforce?

Salesforce is a cloud-based Customer Relationship Management (CRM) platform used to manage:
- Customers
- Sales
- Services
- Marketing
- Business processes

It helps organizations automate workflows and improve customer relationships.

---

# 🔹 Key Concepts Learned

##  1. Cloud Computing
- Salesforce works on cloud technology.
- Users can access data from anywhere using the internet.
- No need to install software locally.

---

##  2. CRM (Customer Relationship Management)

# 🔹 Salesforce Data Model Basics

##  What is a Data Model?
A data model defines how data is stored, connected, and managed inside Salesforce.

---

## Standard Objects
Predefined objects provided by Salesforce:
- Account
- Contact
- Lead
- Opportunity

### Example:
- Account → Company information
- Contact → Customer details

---

##  Custom Objects
Objects created by users based on business requirements.

Example:
- Student
- Hospital
- Library
- Employee

---

##  Fields
Fields store information inside objects.

Examples:
- Name
- Email
- Phone Number
- Address

---

##  Relationships in Salesforce

### 1. Lookup Relationship
- Connects two objects loosely.

### 2. Master-Detail Relationship
- Strong parent-child relationship.
- Child depends on parent.
