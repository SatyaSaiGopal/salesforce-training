# Business flow

# Lead

A Lead is a person who shows interest in a company’s product or service for the first time.

At this stage:

The company has only basic information.

The person is not yet a confirmed customer.

# Contact

A Contact is a qualified person whose details have been verified and with whom the company has started communication.

The business now knows:

Name

Phone number

Email

Requirements

# Opportunity

An Opportunity represents a potential business deal or service that may generate revenue.

It tracks:

Product or service

Estimated amount

Progress of the deal

Chance of success

# Customer

A Customer is a person who successfully purchases the product or uses the service.

Once the deal is completed:

Payment is done

Service is delivered

Relationship becomes official.



# Real life mapping of Hospital management

Account = Hospital Organization

Contact = Patient

Lead = Interested Patient

Opportunity = Treatment or Service Deal
