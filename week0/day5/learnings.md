# End of Day Outcomes

* **Language Fundamentals:** Acquired a solid understanding of Apex syntax, including the role of Classes, Methods, and Variables.
* **Database Management:** Mastered the use of DML (Data Manipulation Language) for record creation and SOQL (Salesforce Object Query Language) for precise data retrieval.
* **Contextual Awareness:** Learned to identify the "Execution Context," ensuring code runs efficiently within the shared cloud environment.
* **Architectural Strategy:** Developed the ability to evaluate a business problem and decide whether a Flow or an Apex trigger is the most sustainable solution.
