# Reflective Analysis

## **1. Why is Apex needed if Salesforce already has Flows?**
Apex allows for deeper customization, such as complex triggers, custom REST APIs, and high-performance processing that visual tools cannot execute.

## **2. When should developers prefer no-code solutions?**
No-code solutions should be the default for 90% of tasks. They are easier for future administrators to understand, require less testing, and update automatically with Salesforce releases.

## **3. What problems require custom programming?**
Problems involving external system integrations, complex mathematical algorithms, or scenarios requiring specialized user interface components.

## **4. Why is business logic important in enterprise systems?**
Business logic acts as the "guardrails" for an organization, ensuring that data is uniform, processes are followed, and human error is minimized.

## **5. Why should developers avoid unnecessary coding?**
Coding creates "Technical Debt." Every line of code must be manually maintained, updated, and tested, which increases the total cost of ownership for the system.

## **6. How does programming increase flexibility?**
Programming removes the constraints of the standard UI, allowing developers to create entirely custom workflows and experiences tailored to a specific user's unique needs.
